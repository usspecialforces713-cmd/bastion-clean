Ronograd.

We remember the sacrifices.

The long patrols through hostile streets. The desperate last stands. The missions where survival depended not on individual strength, but on trust, teamwork, and the willingness to put others before yourself.

Some operators fought for glory. Others fought for their squad. But the greatest among them understood that no medal, no rank, and no victory was worth more than the lives of the people standing beside them.

Because a battlefield is not remembered for its buildings. It is remembered for the people who fought on it.

We came as individuals, but we fought as brothers and sisters in arms.

And even as we move on to new deployments, new battlefields, and new challenges, a part of us will always remain in those streets.

**The map may be gone, but the legacy lives on.**

Rest easy, old Ronograd.

Your watch is over.
